About ClutchBids

Australia's premier marketplace for car enthusiasts to buy and sell extraordinary vehicles.

Our Mission

ClutchBids was founded with a singular goal: to create a transparent and trustworthy marketplace where Australian car enthusiasts can confidently buy and sell vehicles. We're passionate about cars and believe every transaction should be built on trust, clarity, and community.

Every listing is reviewed and verified by our team before going live, ensuring you only see genuine cars from real sellers. Our offer system keeps negotiations fair, secure, and drama-free.

How It Works

1. Verify your identity

Upload your ID for a one-time verification. It keeps our marketplace safe for everyone.

2. List your car

Create a detailed listing with photos, specs, and your asking price. Our team reviews it promptly.

3. Negotiate & Close

Receive offers, negotiate via our secure chat, and seal the deal with confidence.
